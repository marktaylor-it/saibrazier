One of three businesses

Coaching is not a side interest borrowed from somewhere else.

run2live is Sai Brazier’s endurance exercise coaching work. It sits alongside BeLingo in EdTech and Eye of Faith in media publication as the third of the three businesses he builds.

Sai is an athlete himself. He counts it among the handful of words he uses to describe his own life, next to student, entrepreneur, podcaster, writer, and man of faith. It is the same conviction, applied to the body.

It starts with watching how you move

Before any of this was a business, it was a job on a shop floor.

At Columbus Running Company in Ohio, Sai fit customers for running shoes. The process began with observation: watching a person’s walking mechanics, reading what their body was actually doing, and then offering shoe options tailored to their anatomical needs. Not the newest model. Not the one in their size. The one their mechanics asked for.

He handled the unglamorous half too, tracking purchases through the store system, pulling inventory from other Columbus locations, placing special orders, and ordering from vendors when what a runner needed was not on the wall.

That sequence still governs the coaching. Observation before prescription. Mechanics before mileage. And if the right answer takes an extra step to find, go find it.

How Sai coaches

  1. 01Curiosity first. Your history, your injuries, your schedule, your reasons — asked before anything is written down.
  2. 02Limits are a starting point, not a verdict. The ceiling you assume you have is the first thing worth testing.
  3. 03Learn from the people who have gone further. Their successes, their adversities, and the wisdom they acquired along the way.
  4. 04Purpose before performance. Training is one more place to spend a life well, not a number to chase for its own sake.
