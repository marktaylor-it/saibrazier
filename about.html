In my own words

I thrive on unrelenting ambition driven by intense curiosity.

Embracing the impossible and pushing the limits is my natural response to any challenge or business endeavor. If it’s possible, I’ll do it. If not, I’ll find a way to make it possible.

I enjoy learning from others: their successes, their adversities, and the wisdom they acquired along the way. Life is rich with opportunity to do good, and I am here simply to make the most of it.

What I’ve learned from the people I’ve interviewed

As a student, entrepreneur, podcaster, writer, athlete, and man of faith, I have been blessed with several opportunities to interview and associate with well accomplished men and women of faith across the world.

As I have studied their lives, I’ve noticed a nonnegotiable commonality they all possess: a genuine zeal for life and a desire to fulfill their life’s mission.

That intrinsic sense of duty to accomplish all I possibly can in this life instills purpose and a sense of responsibility into all I do.
