My history in podcast production

From May 2024 to February 2026, on a service mission for The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, Sai produced, hosted and general-managed a podcast run by a sub-team of service volunteers.

The same role had him leading a national team of volunteer content creators. Two years of running a show with volunteers, on a volunteer schedule, is a specific kind of training. It teaches you that the interview is the easy part.

Photo

If you know someone whose life would make a good conversation, say so. That is usually how the next episode starts.
